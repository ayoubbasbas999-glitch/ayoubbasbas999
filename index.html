What is needed for a real launch?

  • Hosted backend and database for shared accounts, posts, teams, comments, and XP.
  • Secure authentication, database access rules, and account recovery.
  • Server-side moderation tools, reporting queues, and abuse prevention.
  • Real-time messaging with blocking and safety controls.
  • Hosted tournament schedules, brackets, and verified match results.
  • Marketplace hosting and legal/payment review before any transactions.
  • Website hosting, domain, backups, and ongoing maintenance.
